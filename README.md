# Legal Case Dataset for Artificial Intelligence Research

## 1. Overview

This repository provides a structured legal dataset developed to support research and development in **Artificial Intelligence (AI), Natural Language Processing (NLP), Machine Learning (ML), and Legal AI applications**.

The dataset comprises different legal documents including pleadings, applications, judicial orders, and final judgments.

It is intended to facilitate research in legal document.

In addition to the dataset, this repository provides the associated published research paper, Python source code, required libraries, and trained machine learning models to support research reproducibility and further development.

## 3. Dataset Description

The dataset consists of six categories of legal documents representing different stages of litigation and judicial proceedings.

| Document Category | Description |
|---|---|
| **Plaint** | Documents containing the plaintiff's claims, facts, legal grounds, and relief sought before the court. |
| **Written Statement** | Responses submitted by defendants addressing the allegations and claims raised in the plaint. |
| **Replication** | Documents containing the plaintiff's response to the defendant's written statement. |
| **Application** | Applications submitted by parties seeking specific judicial directions, relief, or procedural orders. |
| **Order** | Interim, procedural, and other judicial orders passed by the court during proceedings. |
| **Judgment** | Judicial decisions containing case facts, legal issues, arguments, reasoning, and final conclusions. |

These document categories collectively provide a representation of different stages of litigation, enabling research into relationships between pleadings, judicial proceedings, and judicial outcomes.
